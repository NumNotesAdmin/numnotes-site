The free version and the full version

The free version opens 4 songs. You choose which: when one of them is deleted, tap a locked song to open it in the free place. The full version is a monthly or yearly subscription. It opens every song, and lets you share and print scores as NumNotes files, PDF or MIDI.

Restore a subscription

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings › App › Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Change or cancel a subscription

In NumNotes, open Settings › App › Subscription › Manage Subscription. You can also use the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad: tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you cancel, your songs are kept; after the subscription ends, 4 of them stay open.

Back up your songs

Open Settings › Backup and tap Back up all songs… to save your songs, setlists and tags as one backup file. Keep it in Files or iCloud Drive; to restore, open the file in NumNotes.

Use NumNotes on more than one device

Turn on Settings › App › iCloud Sync on each device signed in to the same Apple Account. Your library stays in step through your own iCloud.
