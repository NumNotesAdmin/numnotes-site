Privacy Policy · English

NumNotes does not collect any personal data. It has no account of its own, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer receives nothing about you or your songs.

Your songs, setlists and settings are kept on this device, in the app’s own storage. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they are also kept in your own iCloud account, through Apple, so your devices stay in step. The developer cannot see them.

Subscriptions are bought and managed with your Apple Account. Apple handles the payment and tells NumNotes only whether a subscription is active.

A song leaves NumNotes only when you share or export it yourself — for example by AirDrop, Messages or Save to Files. From then on, the app or service you send it to handles it.

Deleting NumNotes deletes everything it has stored on this device. Songs kept in iCloud stay there until you delete them.
